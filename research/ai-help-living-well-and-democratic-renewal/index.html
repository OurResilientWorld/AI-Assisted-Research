Case study 04 · Three-report research series

AI Help, Living Well and Democratic Renewal

Can Australia's existing systems manage interacting risks to 2035, where is AI necessary or useful, and can broad AI-enabled civic resilience be delivered safely and durably?

What is being tested: A three-stage test of the strongest feasible non-AI comparator, AI necessity and human benefit, and the delivery, safeguards and endurance of broad AI-enabled civic resilience.

  • Author: Rick Molony, Our Resilient World
  • Research cut-off: 22 August 2026
  • Three reports
  • CC BY 4.0

Cumulative portfolio verdict

UNRESOLVED

No function is demonstrated NECESSARY. Anticipation and forecasting, and cyber and AI-threat management, are USEFUL NOT NECESSARY. Climate, infrastructure and logistics; care and living well; information judgement; and deliberation remain UNRESOLVED. P2 is the most credible near-term delivery pattern but remains partial; P3 and P4 remain unproven.

Boundary: This is not a recommendation, forecast or claim that AI is either required generally or useless. It preserves a strong non-AI comparator and keeps missing delivery, safeguard, ecological, distributional and democratic evidence visible.

Three-stage test

The argument is tested in sequence

  1. 1
    Stage 1

    Whether Australia's existing systems and strongest feasible non-AI reforms are adequate.

  2. 2
    Stage 2

    Whether AI is necessary or only useful, and whether it helps Australians live and decide well.

  3. 3
    Stage 3

    Whether broad AI-enabled civic resilience can be delivered, safeguarded and sustained.

Accepted reports

Report 1 — Can Australia’s Existing Systems Manage the Interacting Risks to 2035?

COMPARATOR GAP, NOT AI NECESSITY. The launch baseline plus the strongest feasible non-AI reform package is not demonstrated adequate as a whole. That comparator gap does not establish that AI is necessary.

Read Report 1 in full

Report 2 — Where Is AI Necessary and Can It Help Australians Live and Decide Well?

NO FUNCTION DEMONSTRATED NECESSARY. Anticipation and forecasting, and cyber and AI-driven threat management, are useful but not necessary. The other four functions and every function's mandatory gate state remain unresolved.

Read Report 2 in full

Report 3 — Can Broad AI-Enabled Civic Resilience Work by 2035?

UNRESOLVED. Broad AI-enabled civic resilience is not yet shown to be deliverable, safeguarded and sustainable by 2035. P2 is the clearest bounded delivery case, but no pathway passes the whole system.
