# Contributing to AI-Assisted Research

Contributions should make the public evidence record more accurate, testable or accessible.

## Choose the right route

- **Correction:** identify an exact passage, defect and proposed correction.
- **New evidence:** provide a public source that may strengthen, weaken or qualify a finding.
- **Research proposal:** propose a testable method for an accepted evidence gap.
- **Accessibility:** report a barrier in navigation, reading order, contrast or alternatives.

## Submission standard

Include the source URL, exact locator, publication date, relevant population and method, and a stated
limitation. Distinguish creation, displacement and augmentation where relevant. Do not treat an
activity count as proof of viable income, survival or distribution.

GitHub issues are public. Do not submit confidential information, personal information about other
people, credentials, defamatory material or content you do not have permission to share.

## Review and decisions

Triage classifies a submission; it does not accept evidence or alter a published result. Typographic
changes may receive a patch update. Changes to evidence, confidence, method, pathway, hypothesis or
verdict require research review and Product Owner acceptance.

Be respectful and evidence-focused. Do not harass, discriminate, dox, threaten or misrepresent
another contributor's evidence. Maintainers may close, hide or report material that breaches these
rules or GitHub's policies.
