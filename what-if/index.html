What If Research

Research that begins from clearly labelled design premises and investigates what responsible implementation would require.

  • Research date: 9 September 2026
  • Research by Rick Molony
  • Developed with AI assistance

Evidence and premises remain separate. A What If proposition is not an observed fact. Designed success is neither proof nor a forecast, and the alternative pathways carry no assigned probabilities.
