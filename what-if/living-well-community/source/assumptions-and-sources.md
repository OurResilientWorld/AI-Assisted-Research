## Method adaptation

Credit: State of Victoria, Department of Treasury and Finance, *Investment Lifecycle and High Value High Risk Guidelines — Business Case*, copyright 2023, downloadable 2024 file, accessed 17November 2026; licensedCCBY3.0Australia except specified branding/images. The proposal uses independently written text, not government branding or a reproduced template.

The practical adaptation is to state the problem and outcomes first; compare current services and three feasible delivery arrangements; explain governance, procurement, budget and schedule; allocate benefit owners and measurement; and finish with a viability/decision assessment. A short community-led pilot warrants proportionate analysis rather than state infrastructure submission machinery. National government funding gates, ministerial sign-off and public financing examples are not requirements for this proposal. No state endorsement or completed facilitated workshop is implied.

The taxonomy overview informs the proposed governance approach. Its four powers are proposed for this organisation within lawful duties, not claimed as new statutory powers over public services. The proposed 30-day emergency review is a programme design choice. 

## Four-tab presentation and new Living Well organisation

The presentation adapts the editorial hierarchy, tabs, optional detail and read-all/print pattern of the [Product Owners AI Guide](https://aipf.ourresilient.world/guides/product-owners-ai-guide.html).

Current governance recommendation: a NSW incorporated association, inferred from the community purpose and One World principles, subject to legal eligibility advice. Official [NSW association guidance](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/incorporated-associations/about-incorporated-associations), [registration requirements](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/apply-to-register-an-incorporated-association), [committee duties](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/incorporated-associations/running-an-incorporated-association/management-committee-obligations) and [resolutions](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/incorporated-associations/running-an-incorporated-association/resolutions) were checked on 17 November 2026. Current legislation endpoint returned 403; no claim of full statutory review. D1/D2 now represent base/lower overhead for the same association, with numeric inputs unchanged and no legal-form saving inferred.

Living Well's full device/app/cloud/AI/support chain requires privacy, accessibility, clinical and continuity checks, including the retained non-Chinese model-provider requirement. Association formation, charity registration, deductible gifts, fundraising compliance and name availability are separate questions. No supplier or partner commitment is claimed.
