# Disruption tests and continuity arrangements

## Financial and service stress tests

The table tests **250 new places with common hypothetical recurring member/sponsor receipts, in 2026 dollars**, under each delivery structure. It is an after-pilot pricing stress, not an account of pilot donations. Costs include 10% reserve; setup is excluded. Negative balance means annual receipts fall short. These deterministic scenarios have no assigned probabilities and do not demonstrate service performance.

## Five disruptions tested in every option

| Disruption | Cost/receipt assumption | Practical response and community decision | Failure criterion / owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workforce shortage |30% rise in support/onboarding allowances: $600/person before reserve; broad cost proxy, not measured wages/hours | Secure paid professional cover; reduce proposed intake if needed; members set priorities within lawful and safe clinical capacity; committee manages execution and urgent protective action | Cannot roster safe care, or nearby unmet need rises due to staff diversion; clinical lead |
| Heatwave/bushfire | $150/person alternative access plus $15,000 response coordination before reserve | Suspend unsafe activity, contact high-need people, use suitable accessible alternatives; executive action under agreed plan and automatic committee review and member reporting | High-need people unreachable, unsafe resumption or essential support lost; continuity lead |
| Higher support needs/ageing | Additional 20% need $1,500 more support: $300 averaged across places before reserve | Reassess needs and costs, fund safe transition beyond 79; committee revisits access/budget with member input, not individual treatment | Necessary support rationed or burden shifted involuntarily to families; clinical/access leads |
| Recession/payer exit |25% recurring receipts lost at unchanged committed costs; separate removal of sponsor receipts | Monitor cash and cancellations; committee revises plans while protecting current obligations; freeze new intake before commitments exceed funding | Cash below funded continuity requirement with no enforceable replenishment; finance lead |
| Technology/power/supplier disruption | $300/person response plus $10,000 shared response before reserve | Printed plans, paid telephone/human contact and safe pause; restore and verify controlled data systems | Lost safe contact, unverified data containment or unavailable fallback staff; privacy/clinical leads |

Living Well’s committee must secure paid capacity and reserves under either legal form. Management has delegated emergency authority; it does not wait for a membership vote to protect people. Independent clinical stop authority, automatic committee review and appropriate member reporting apply. A nonprofit label does not itself supply staff or money.

## Startup versus renewal shocks

Before startup, campaign shortfall or delayed clearance prevents launch. The fully funded pilot therefore has no assumed member/sponsor receipt exposure during its first year. It still faces cost shocks. The monthly pilot CSV tests workforce and outage costs against the cash buffer, showing whether that cushion would be consumed. A positive closing balance does not excuse a breached reserve policy or prove professionals exist.

After the pilot, affordability, sponsor/insurer termination and donor fatigue affect renewal. No ordinary government rescue or emergency appeal is assumed. Startup donors’ restrictions may prohibit using residual money for expansion; obtain consent or return funds under agreed terms. Reject unsafe service reductions made only to balance a forecast.

## Other sensitivities and compound failures

Rural delivery adds $600/person; adverse AI response adds $300/person and $50,000 shared incident cost before reserve. No-health-gain leaves contract receipts unchanged initially, demonstrating that a financial result can coexist with failed benefits. Lower/upper cost allowances, no payer and donor renewal cessation are separate tests. Joint workforce, heat and funding loss could be worse than any single row; commission a combined continuity budget before committing to expansion rather than inventing a joint probability.

Outcome tests record essential support maintained, missed contacts, recovery time, cost, carer burden and who carried losses. Neither a funded drill nor an annual surplus establishes national resilience.
