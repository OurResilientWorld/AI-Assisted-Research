Accessibility

Report a barrier in the website or publication material

GitHub account and sign-in required

You will write and submit your feedback on GitHub. Sign in when prompted, or create a GitHub account before continuing.

Feedback and comments are public. Do not include confidential, sensitive or personal information.

Open accessibility form on GitHub

The form opens in a new tab. Write your answers there, review them, then select Submit new issue. Opening the form does not submit anything.

What the GitHub form asks
  • Page or content location — required
  • Barrier type — required
  • What happened and what did you expect? — required
  • Browser, device or assistive technology (optional) — optional
  • I have not included confidential, sensitive or personal information. (required)

What happens next?

When you submit, GitHub creates a public issue in the AI-Assisted Research repository. You can return to its page to read replies and continue the discussion. Your submission does not automatically appear on this website.

Maintainers review the issue and may ask for clarification. Submission does not accept evidence or change a published result. Changes to evidence, confidence, method or findings require research review and Product Owner acceptance.
