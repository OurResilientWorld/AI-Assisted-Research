# Community conduct

AI-Assisted Research welcomes respectful, evidence-focused participation.

## Expected behaviour

- address claims and methods rather than attacking people;
- state interests, uncertainty and limitations that may affect a submission;
- protect privacy and do not post confidential or personal information;
- do not harass, discriminate, threaten, dox or deliberately misrepresent another person;
- follow GitHub's Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policies.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit labels, request clarification, close or hide contributions, restrict
participation, or use GitHub's reporting process. Serious safety or abuse concerns should be reported
through GitHub's private report-abuse mechanism rather than posted as a public issue.

Moderation is administrative. It does not determine whether a research claim is accepted.
